## Adapter and coverage-check design

A manifest should name the official source URL/domain, supported format (JSON-LD Event, iCalendar or a reviewed site-specific extraction recipe), season/date bounds and known venue mapping. The shared adapter retains only actually published occurrence dates, event-specific URLs, cancellation/estimated-time status and explicit free/admission qualifiers. Apply request/page/result budgets, existing retry/health telemetry and stable provider occurrence identities. Generic season text does not become a repeating weekly series without dated source evidence.

The independent coverage report accepts a bounded set of official occurrence expectations with evidence URLs, expected dates and freshness, and compares those exact title/date/venue identities against the database. States: covered, missing while corpus fresh, corpus stale, expectation stale, and off-season/outside ingest horizon. Generic umbrella rows and dev seeds cannot satisfy a dated occurrence. Missing credentials or an inactive season must remain distinguishable from parse failure. Run on demand first; scheduling or live scraping is not activated here.

The on-demand check is now implemented in `scripts/audit_civic_coverage.py` with a reviewed occurrence manifest in `docs/civic-expectations-2026-09-29.json`. The manifest records actual official event-specific dates for African Arts Festival (October 3), Quinteto Latino (October 8), Halloween Hoopla (October 31), the announced 2027 Nihonmachi date outside the near-term horizon, and a Golden Gate Park Band season reference with no invented future occurrence. The three Yerba Buena event pages were rechecked September 29; links and check time are in the manifest. The manifest is a bounded canary, not an exhaustive calendar.

Run `PYTHONPATH=. DATABASE_URL=... .venv/bin/python scripts/audit_civic_coverage.py --manifest docs/civic-expectations-2026-09-29.json`. Reads enforce a PostgreSQL READ ONLY transaction and a statement timeout, select only public catalog/source-health fields, cap the horizon at 365 days and refuse snapshots exceeding 10,000 rows. No provider calls, scraping or scheduling occurs. Exit codes: 0 covered/inactive dates; 1 a missing dated expectation with fresh complete evidence from every named coverage path; 2 input/database-read failure; 3 stale or unverified evidence. A source must have a healthy, nonpartial success in the last 24 hours and after the official expectation check. Existing matches may be reported covered with stale-source diagnostics, which proves stored representation rather than fresh attendance eligibility.

Fixtures verify separate venue/date identities, SF calendar dates across UTC midnight, umbrella/seed/cancelled exclusions, partial/error/stale/future health evidence, expectation expiry, off-season/past/outside-horizon states and database-write rejection. The adapter recommendation remains research; before implementing/activating an official feed, validate current page format, fetch permissions and fresh deployed counts. This completes the audit, design decision and executable coverage check in #24; it does not establish complete civic coverage or activate new ingestion.
